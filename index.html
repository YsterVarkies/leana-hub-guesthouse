In every stay

  • A private bathroom in every room, with fresh towels and toiletries laid out on the bed.

  • Cool through a Highveld summer afternoon, warm on a frosty winter night.

  • An electric kettle, a tea and coffee tray and a bar fridge for your own milk and drinks.

  • A proper work desk with a lamp, for the conference week, the thesis or the email you brought along.

  • A TV in every room; ask for one of the rooms with a Smart TV if you want your own streaming.

  • A fully stocked kitchen to cook in, a dining area, and the fireplace lounge to sit in afterwards.

  • Fresh fruit picked from the trees in the garden, lemons included, on the table for guests.

  • Free private parking on site, private check-in and check-out, and someone at the desk around the clock.

Good to know

When can I check in? From 14:00 to 22:00. Arriving later? Call ahead on 079 296 2187; the front desk is staffed around the clock.

And check out? Between 07:00 and 10:00.

Can I cook? Yes. Guests share a fully stocked kitchen, the dining area and the lounge, and there is a braai in the garden.

Is the pool open in winter? The outdoor pool is open all year round.

Is there parking? Free private parking on site, and space to lock up bicycles.

Is there Wi-Fi? Free Wi-Fi in the rooms and the shared spaces.

Can I bring the children? Children of any age are welcome. There are no cots or extra beds, so a family of three or four takes the Protea or the Pine Room.

Pets? Not at this house, sorry.

Smoking? The house and rooms are non-smoking.

Parties? No parties or events, hen and stag parties included. Quiet hours run from 22:00 to 08:00.

Can you fetch me? A shuttle can be arranged at a charge. Ask when you book.

How do cancellations work? Cancellation and prepayment depend on the rate you choose on Booking.com, and are shown before you pay.

Call or WhatsApp us about dates, late arrivals or directions.
